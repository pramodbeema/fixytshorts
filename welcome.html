The ultimate YouTube Shorts experience enhancer.

🔁

Hands-Free Auto-Scroll

Click the Auto button on Shorts to auto-advance to the next Short when finished.

⚡

Playback Speed

Press > or < (or Shift + . / ,) to adjust playback rate from 0.25x to 2.0x.

🎧

Audio Track Navigator

Press 'a' for audio menu with full keyboard selection (or press again to cycle).

⏩

Shorts Seeker

Use ← → to seek with customizable duration set in popup.

🔄

Shorts to Video

Press Ctrl+Shift+F or click the on-screen Convert button.

💬

Caption Toggle

Press 'c' to toggle or navigate captions with keyboard.

⏮️

Restart Video

Press '0' to restart from the beginning.
